UTC+3 includes Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Kenya, and Russia (western portions). Turkey permanently adopted UTC+3 year-round in 2016. Most countries here do not use DST. Deserts, savannas, and mountain regions dominate geographically. Islam strongly shapes cultural and social life across much of the region. Coffee culture in Ethiopia and Kenya has global influence. Historically, this zone contains former centers of the Ottoman Empire, Islamic caliphates, and ancient trade routes linking Africa, Asia, and Europe.
