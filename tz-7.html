UTC+7 includes Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, and western Indonesia. DST is generally not observed. Tropical climates, monsoon forests, and major river systems define the region. Buddhism is culturally dominant in Thailand and Cambodia, while Vietnam reflects Confucian, Buddhist, and socialist influences. Cuisine emphasizes rice, herbs, seafood, and street food traditions.
