UTC+12 includes New Zealand and Fiji. New Zealand observes DST, shifting to UTC+13 during summer. Fiji has periodically used DST. The region features mountains, fjords, volcanic islands, and subtropical beaches. Māori culture is central to New Zealand's national identity. Rugby, sheep farming, and film production are major cultural and economic features. Because these zones are among the first to enter a new calendar day, they hold symbolic significance during New Year celebrations.
