UTC+9 includes Japan and South Korea. Neither currently observes DST. Tokyo and Seoul are major global technology and cultural centers. Japan is known for anime, Shinto traditions, and advanced transportation systems. South Korea is globally influential through K-pop, cinema, and electronics manufacturing. Seasonal festivals, seafood cuisine, and Confucian social traditions remain culturally important.
