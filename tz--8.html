UTC-8 includes western United States and Canada, including cities such as Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, and Vancouver. Most regions observe DST, shifting to UTC-7 during summer. However, some nearby jurisdictions, like Arizona, do not. The Pacific coast combines temperate rainforests, Mediterranean climates, mountains, and deserts. Economically, this zone is globally influential due to Silicon Valley, Hollywood, aerospace industries, and Pacific Rim trade. Culturally, the region is associated with technological innovation, environmental activism, multicultural immigration, and entertainment industries. Indigenous nations throughout the Pacific Northwest maintain strong artistic traditions involving totem carving and salmon-centered cultural practices. Historically, Pacific Time expanded with westward U.S. settlement and railway standardization during the 19th century.
