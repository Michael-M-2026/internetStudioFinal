UTC-5 covers eastern North America, including cities such as New York, Washington D.C., Toronto, Miami, and Havana. Most regions observe DST, shifting to UTC-4 in summer. The climate ranges from Canadian winters to subtropical Florida beaches. Financial markets, media industries, and government institutions make this one of the world's most economically influential time zones. Cultural life includes Broadway theater, Caribbean music traditions, major sports leagues, and immigrant communities from around the world. Cuisine ranges from Cuban dishes in Florida to French-Canadian influences in eastern Canada. Historically, this zone became the center of U.S. political and financial power, hosting Wall Street and the federal capital.
