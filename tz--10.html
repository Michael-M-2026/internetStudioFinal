UTC-10 is primarily used by Hawaii and parts of Alaska's Aleutian Islands, as well as territories in the central Pacific such as French Polynesia. Major cities include Honolulu and Papeete. Hawaii does not observe DST, largely because its tropical latitude provides relatively consistent daylight year-round. Parts of Alaska using this offset do observe DST seasonally. The region is dominated by volcanic islands, coral reefs, and Pacific Ocean ecosystems. Hawaii's geography ranges from tropical beaches to snow-capped volcanic peaks on Mauna Kea. Polynesian navigation traditions shaped settlement patterns long before European arrival. Culturally, Native Hawaiian customs remain influential through hula, surfing, chants, and respect for ancestral land stewardship. Tourism, military infrastructure, and Pacific trade are central economic forces. French Polynesia contributes strong Polynesian-French cultural blending, including Tahitian dance, tattoo traditions, and maritime economies. Historically, Hawaii's annexation by the United States transformed the islands into a strategic Pacific hub. Pearl Harbor remains globally significant in military history.
