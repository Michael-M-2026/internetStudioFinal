UTC-4 includes eastern Canada, parts of the Caribbean, and northern South America. Cities include Halifax, San Juan, and Caracas. DST practices vary: Canadian Atlantic provinces generally observe DST, while most Caribbean nations do not. The region includes Atlantic coastlines, tropical islands, rainforests, and mountain ranges. Fishing, tourism, shipping, and petroleum industries are important. Cultural diversity is immense. Caribbean societies blend African, Indigenous, European, and Asian influences through music, religion, and cuisine. Carnival celebrations and calypso music are internationally recognized. Historically, the Caribbean's colonial plantation economies shaped global trade and migration patterns.
