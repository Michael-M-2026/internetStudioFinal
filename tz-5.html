UTC+5 includes Pakistan, Uzbekistan, and Maldives. Major cities include Karachi, Tashkent, and Malé. DST is uncommon. Geography ranges from Himalayan foothills to deserts and tropical atolls. Islam is the majority religion across most countries here. Central Asian architecture reflects Persian, Turkic, and Soviet influence. Tourism and fisheries dominate the Maldives economy. Historically, Silk Road trade routes crossed this zone extensively.
