UTC+4 includes United Arab Emirates, Armenia, Georgia, and parts of Russia. DST is generally not observed. Dubai and Abu Dhabi are major global aviation and finance hubs. The region combines desert climates with mountainous Caucasus terrain. Oil wealth transformed Gulf economies into international business centers. Armenian and Georgian cultures preserve ancient Christian traditions and unique alphabets.
