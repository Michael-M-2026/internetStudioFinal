Cities: South Georgia, Azores West, Atlantic Islands

UTC-2 is sparsely populated and mainly used by remote Atlantic territories such as parts of Brazil's offshore islands and former South Atlantic observances. These isolated islands often have strategic scientific or military importance. Harsh oceanic weather and limited infrastructure define daily life. Because populations are small, DST practices are generally minimal or absent. Economies depend on research stations, fishing, and environmental monitoring.
