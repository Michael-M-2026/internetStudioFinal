UTC-9 covers most of Alaska. Major cities include Anchorage, Fairbanks, and Juneau. Alaska observes DST, shifting to UTC-8 during summer months to maximize evening daylight. The geography is immense and rugged: glaciers, tundra, mountain ranges, and Arctic coastlines dominate the landscape. Winters bring extreme darkness in northern areas, while summers feature the “midnight sun.” Alaska Native cultures—including Inuit, Yupik, and Athabaskan peoples—maintain traditions involving fishing, storytelling, carving, and subsistence hunting. Russian influence also survives in architecture and Orthodox Christian communities because Alaska was once part of the Russian Empire before its 1867 purchase by the United States. Oil extraction, fishing, and tourism are major industries. Climate change strongly affects the region through melting permafrost and coastal erosion.
