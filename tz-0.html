UTC±0 includes the United Kingdom, Ireland, Portugal, and several West African nations. London, Dublin, Lisbon, and Accra are major cities. The UK and Ireland observe DST, becoming UTC+1 during summer. Many African countries remain on standard time year-round. This zone is historically important because Greenwich became the global prime meridian standard during the 19th century. Maritime navigation and British imperial influence spread the system worldwide. Cultural contributions include English literature, football, maritime traditions, and diverse immigrant communities.
