UTC-6 spans central North America, including large portions of United States, Canada, and Mexico. Major cities include Chicago, Dallas, Houston, Winnipeg, and Mexico City. DST is widely observed, though policies differ in parts of Mexico. The region encompasses prairies, Great Plains farmland, forests, and Gulf Coast wetlands. Agriculture, manufacturing, finance, and oil industries are major economic drivers. Culturally, the zone contains major centers for blues, jazz, country, and Tejano music. Barbecue traditions differ sharply by state and country. Indigenous, European, African, and Latin American influences combine throughout the region. Historically, Chicago's railway prominence helped standardize North American timekeeping systems.
