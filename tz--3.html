UTC-3 includes most of Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay, and Chile (seasonally in some regions). Major cities include São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Buenos Aires, and Montevideo. Brazil abolished national DST in 2019, while Chile still uses seasonal DST in many regions. Geography ranges from Amazon rainforest to pampas grasslands and Patagonian plains. Football culture dominates much of the region. Tango originated in Argentina and Uruguay, while samba and Carnival are globally associated with Brazil. Catholicism remains influential, though religious diversity is increasing. Historically, immigration from Europe, Africa, and Indigenous populations created highly mixed societies.
