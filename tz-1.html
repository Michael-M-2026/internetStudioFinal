UTC+1 covers much of western and central Europe, including France, Germany, Italy, and Spain. Most countries observe DST. Major cities include Paris, Berlin, Rome, and Madrid. Geography ranges from Mediterranean coastlines to Alpine mountains and northern plains. The region is known for classical music, Renaissance art, industrial manufacturing, and influential political institutions like the European Union. Cuisine varies dramatically, from Italian pasta traditions to German bread-making and French wine culture. Spain's use of UTC+1 despite its western geography remains politically controversial because it aligns poorly with solar time.
