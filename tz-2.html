UTC+2 includes parts of eastern Europe, the eastern Mediterranean, and southern Africa. Countries include Greece, Finland, Egypt, and South Africa. European countries often observe DST; South Africa does not. Climate varies from Nordic forests to Mediterranean coastlines and African savannas. Orthodox Christianity strongly influences Greece and parts of eastern Europe, while Egypt has deep Islamic and ancient historical traditions. South Africa contributes multilingual cultural diversity shaped by African, European, and Asian heritage.
