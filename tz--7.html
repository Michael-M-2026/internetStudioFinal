UTC-7 includes parts of the western United States, western Canada, and northern Mexico. Major cities include Denver, Phoenix, Calgary, and Edmonton. Most areas observe DST, though Arizona notably remains on standard time year-round except for Navajo Nation territories. The geography includes the Rocky Mountains, deserts, plateaus, and forests. Outdoor recreation, mining, cattle ranching, and energy production dominate many regional economies. Culture varies widely between urban technology centers and rural frontier communities. Indigenous heritage remains strong among Navajo, Apache, and Pueblo peoples. Mexican cultural influence becomes increasingly visible farther south. Historically, this zone developed around railroad scheduling and mining expansion across the American West.
