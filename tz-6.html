UTC+6 includes Bangladesh, Bhutan, and parts of Kazakhstan and Russia. Bangladesh briefly experimented with DST but discontinued it. Monsoon climates, Himalayan mountains, and grasslands dominate the geography. Bengali literature and cuisine are culturally significant. Buddhism strongly shapes Bhutanese identity, alongside policies emphasizing “Gross National Happiness.”
