UTC-1 is primarily used by the Azores. The islands observe DST, shifting to UTC±0 during summer. Located in the Atlantic Ocean, the Azores feature volcanic landscapes, crater lakes, and maritime climates. Portuguese language and Catholic traditions dominate local culture. Fishing, dairy production, tourism, and transatlantic aviation have historically shaped the economy. Due to their Atlantic position, the islands played important roles in naval and air travel history.
